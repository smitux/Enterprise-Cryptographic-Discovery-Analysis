# ECDAT — Research References

This document contains the key research papers, standards, and technical resources that informed the development and proposed direction of ECDAT.

---

## 1. On Criteria and Tooling for Cryptographic Inventories

**Authors:** Nicolai Schmitt, Johanna Henrich, Dominik Heinz, Nouri Alnahawi, Alexander Wiesmaier

**Focus:** Cryptographic inventory criteria and discovery across different cryptographic surfaces, including software, hardware, networks, stored cryptographic material, and source code.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Provides the foundation for cryptographic asset discovery and inventory.

[Research Paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383638084_On_Criteria_and_Tooling_for_Cryptographic_Inventories)

---

## 2. Quantum Threat Timeline

**Authors:** Michele Mosca & Marco Piani

**Organization:** Global Risk Institute

**Focus:** Estimates the potential timeline for cryptographically relevant quantum computers and the resulting threat to current cryptographic systems.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Supports quantum-threat timeline modelling and migration urgency assessment.

[Global Risk Institute — Quantum Threat Timeline](https://globalriskinstitute.org/publication/quantum-threat-timeline/)

---

## 3. Migration to Post-Quantum Cryptography

**Organization:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)

**Focus:** Cryptographic discovery, risk management, PQC migration, interoperability, performance, and migration planning.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Supports the discovery, risk assessment, and migration-planning aspects of the project.

[NIST Migration to Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://www.nccoe.nist.gov/applied-cryptography/migration-to-pqc)

---

## 4. Transitioning Organizations to Post-Quantum Cryptography

**Authors:** David Joseph et al.

**Journal:** Nature, 2022

**Focus:** Organizational strategies, transition timelines, PQC deployment, performance, and hybrid approaches for post-quantum migration.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Provides an organizational perspective for PQC migration and transition planning.

[Read the Paper — Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04623-2)

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04623-2)

---

## 5. Enterprise-Level Cryptographic Agility (ELCA)

**Focus:** Enterprise-scale cryptographic agility, cryptographic configuration, and managing cryptographic changes across large infrastructures.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Provides research context for enterprise cryptographic agility and migration execution.

[Research Source](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22Enterprise-level+Cryptographic+Agility%22+ELCA)

---

## 6. NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards

### FIPS 203 — ML-KEM

**Focus:** Standard for Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism (ML-KEM).

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Reference for post-quantum key establishment recommendations.

[NIST FIPS 203](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final)

### FIPS 204 — ML-DSA

**Focus:** Standard for Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm (ML-DSA).

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Reference for post-quantum digital signature recommendations.

[NIST FIPS 204](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/final)

---

## 7. Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM)

**Organization:** CycloneDX

**Focus:** Structured representation of cryptographic components, algorithms, and related information.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Provides a foundation for representing discovered cryptographic assets as a Cryptographic Bill of Materials.

[CycloneDX — Cryptographic Algorithm Use Case](https://cyclonedx.org/use-cases/cryptographic-algorithm/)

---

## 8. Getting Ready for Post-Quantum Cryptography

**Authors:** Murugiah Souppaya, Tim Polk & William Barker

**Organization:** NIST

**Published:** 2021

**Focus:** Quantum threats, PQC adoption challenges, crypto-agility, digital signatures, key establishment, and migration planning.

**Relevance to ECDAT:** Supports the project's focus on PQC adoption and migration planning.

[NIST Publication](https://www.nist.gov/publications/getting-ready-post-quantum-cryptography-exploring-challenges-associated-adopting-and)

## How These References Inform ECDAT

The references collectively cover the major areas behind ECDAT:

**Cryptographic Discovery**  
→ Cryptographic inventory research

**Cryptographic Inventory / CBOM**  
→ CBOM and inventory standards

**Quantum Risk**  
→ Quantum threat timeline research

**PQC Recommendation**  
→ NIST PQC standards and migration guidance

**Migration Prioritization**  
→ PQC migration and organizational transition research

**Enterprise Crypto-Agility**  
→ Enterprise-level cryptographic agility research

ECDAT brings these areas together into a single workflow for cryptographic discovery, contextual quantum-risk assessment, PQC recommendation, and migration prioritization.
